Competitive Mode & Timing Rules

⏱️
Live Time Recording & Anti-Cheat Pacing

In Competitive Mode, your official live session timer starts on your very first meow. Every second is recorded to prevent pause-cheating (like saying only 1 meow per minute to rest indefinitely). The leaderboard displays your total meows and exact time taken (plus your Meows-Per-Minute pace)!

⏳
1-Minute (60s) Breathing Break Rule

You can pause for up to 60 seconds between meows to catch your breath. If silence continues past 60 seconds, your run officially concludes and is submitted to the leaderboard.

🛑
30-Minute Maximum Safety Cap ("Touch Grass" Limit)

No continuous meowing session can exceed 30 minutes. Once 30:00 is reached, the app automatically stops the run, locks your score, and insists that you go drink water and touch some grass 🌱!

🔄
Safe Independent Reset

If your run isn't your personal best, click "🔄 Reset Run" anytime. It resets your continuous streak to 0 without affecting your all-day Total Daily Meows!
